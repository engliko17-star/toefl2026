Write an Email Task

Everything you need to know about everyday written communication and functional goals.

In this task, you will be given a scenario that a college student may encounter. You will write an email for a specific purpose and for a specific audience.

You will have 7 minutes to plan and write your response.

Write an email that clearly meets the goal:

  • Explain your ideas well and make the email easy to follow.
  • Use correct grammar and vocabulary.

How it looks

Scoring

How Responses Are Scored

Raters judge responses based on semantic fulfillment, grammatical variety, sentence complexity, and overall lexical resource matching real-time writing contexts.
