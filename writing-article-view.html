Comprehensive guides, templates, and core criteria for the new writing tasks.

1. Writing an Academic Discussion

Everything you need to know about the 10-minute academic forum task.

In this task, you will simulate participation in an online academic discussion forum. A professor will pose a question on a specific topic, and two classmates will provide brief responses presenting their opinions.

You have exactly 10 minutes to read the prompt, review your peers' responses, and type your own contribution. A highly successful response should contain at least 100 words.

Key Requirements:

  • State a clear point of view and support it with relevant details.
  • Contribute meaningfully to the ongoing discussion without just copying opinions.
  • Demonstrate syntactic variety and accurate grammar usage.

High-Scoring Template & Strategy

Recommended Essay Structure:

  1. Acknowledge and Transition: Briefly engage with the discussion context or a peer's point.
  2. Clear Thesis: Clearly state your core position.
  3. Elaboration & Example: Deeply explain your unique rationale with strong individual support.
  4. Concluding Impact: A definitive sentence that seals the argument.

2. Write an Email Task

Everything you need to know about everyday written communication.

In this task, you will be given a scenario that a college student may encounter. You will write an email for a specific purpose — such as making a recommendation, giving an invitation, or proposing a solution — and for a specific audience.

You will be expected to understand the context, identify the appropriate tone and level of formality, and organize your message logically. You will have 7 minutes to plan and write your response.

Write an email that clearly meets the goal and follows normal social rules:

  • Explain your ideas well and make the email easy to follow.
  • Use correct grammar and vocabulary.
  • Follow basic writing rules for spelling, punctuation, and capitalization.

Scoring Criteria

How Responses Are Scored

Raters judge the quality of writing based on three core aspects: Task Completion (fully responding to all parts of the prompt), Language Variety (natural use of diverse grammar patterns and contextual vocabulary), and Language Accuracy (correct spelling, punctuation, and smooth syntax transitions).
